# 🟠 Research Sources — "What's the evidence behind this?"

Cite these when a claim is about human behavior, usability outcomes, or measured effectiveness — never state "users prefer X" or "this improves conversion" without one of these behind it.

---

```yaml
name: Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g)
url: https://www.nngroup.com/
type: research-body
authority: high
topics: [usability, ux-research, ia, psychology, accessibility, ai-ux]
best_for: The single most load-bearing source for this platform — UX laws, usability heuristics, research methodology. Cross-check its claims against Baymard or ACM when the claim is a specific statistic.
source_priority: 1
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: NN/g Articles
url: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/
type: research-body
authority: high
topics: [usability, ux-patterns, research-summaries]
best_for: Article-level citations — link the specific article, not just the domain.
source_priority: 1
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: Baymard Institute
url: https://baymard.com/
type: research-body
authority: high
topics: [ecommerce-ux, usability-testing, benchmarks]
best_for: E-commerce-specific UX research with actual usability-testing data behind it — strong for checkout/form-flow claims (relevant to the bKash/mobile-payment scope noted in this workspace's other project, but general findings apply here too).
source_priority: 1
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: MeasuringU
url: https://measuringu.com/
type: research-body
authority: high
topics: [ux-metrics, sus, usability-statistics]
best_for: The statistical/measurement side of usability claims (SUS scores, sample-size reasoning) — use when a claim needs a "how do we know" answer.
source_priority: 2
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: ACM Digital Library
url: https://dl.acm.org/
type: academic
authority: high
topics: [hci, academic-research, primary-papers]
best_for: The original peer-reviewed paper behind a named law or effect (e.g. Fitts's original 1954 paper, Hick's 1952 paper) — cite the paper directly where possible instead of a secondary summary.
source_priority: 1
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: ACM CHI Conference
url: https://chi.acm.org/
type: academic
authority: high
topics: [hci, academic-research, conference-proceedings]
best_for: The most current peer-reviewed HCI research — useful for the "Emerging" classification in AI UX / Emerging Interaction content, since CHI papers are often the first place a pattern gets named.
source_priority: 2
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```

```yaml
name: Google Scholar
url: https://scholar.google.com/
type: academic
authority: high
topics: [literature-search, citation-discovery]
best_for: Finding the original paper behind a claim when the direct ACM/journal link isn't at hand — a discovery tool, not a source to cite directly.
source_priority: 3
last_checked: 2026-09-10
```
